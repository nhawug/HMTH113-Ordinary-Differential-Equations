Teaching notes, examples, exercises, and resources for an introductory course in Ordinary Differential Equations.

## 🎓 About the Course

This repository contains teaching materials for HMTH113 Ordinary Differential Equations, authored by G. Nhawu for the Department of Mathematics. The course introduces the theory, methods, and applications of ODEs, emphasizing conceptual understanding, problem-solving techniques, and real-world mathematical modeling.

## 📖 Course Curriculum

*   **Chapter 1: Review and Collection of Formulas**
*   **Chapter 2: Introduction to Differential Equations**
*   **Chapter 3: First Order Differential Equations** (Separable, Exact, Linear, Bernoulli, and Applications)
*   **Chapter 4: Higher Order Differential Equations** (Wronskian, Undetermined Coefficients, Variation of Parameters, Cauchy-Euler)
*   **Chapter 5: Existence and Uniqueness of Solutions**
*   **Chapter 6: Series Solutions** (Ordinary Points, Singular Points, Method of Frobenius)
*   **Chapter 7: The Laplace Transform** (Translation Theorems, Unit Step Function, Convolution)
*   **Chapter 8: Systems of First-Order Differential Equations**

## 🧠 Learning Approach

The materials combine mathematical theory, worked examples, exercises, and applications. Students are encouraged to understand the underlying concepts and methods rather than simply memorize solution procedures.
